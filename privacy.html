Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how the ClipCraft video-clipping tool ("the Tool") handles data. The Tool is a personal application used by its owner to publish their own video content.

1. Data We Access

When you authorize the Tool with TikTok, it requests only the permissions needed to identify your account (user.info.basic) and to upload videos to your account's inbox as drafts (video.upload). The Tool does not read your posts, messages, followers, or browsing activity.

2. How Data Is Used

Access tokens issued by TikTok are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are used solely to upload the owner's own video clips. Tokens are never sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party other than TikTok's official API.

3. Data Storage and Retention

All video files, transcripts, and authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine. No user data is stored on external servers operated by the Tool. Tokens can be revoked at any time from your TikTok account settings, which immediately ends the Tool's access.

4. Data Sharing

The Tool does not share personal data with third parties. The only external service it communicates with is TikTok's official API, and only to perform the uploads you request.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the Tool's owner at the contact address associated with the connected account.
